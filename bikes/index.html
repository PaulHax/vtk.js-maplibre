Developer notes

One shared WebGL canvas, using manageCanvas: false and vtkGlyph3DMapper. The feed refreshes each minute.

Height uses 4 meters per bike for visibility. Zero bikes appear as a thin disc. Inactive stations and reports more than 15 minutes older than the feed are omitted.

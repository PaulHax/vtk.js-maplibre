Radar data source and reuse

This directory contains a processed subset of NOAA/National Weather Service
NEXRAD Level II observations from KTLX, Oklahoma, acquired on 20 May 2013
from 20:16:43 to 20:20:50 UTC. NSF Unidata distributes the source archive:
https://unidata-nexrad-level2.s3.amazonaws.com/2013/05/20/KTLX/KTLX20130520_201643_V06.gz
Dataset citation: https://doi.org/10.7289/V5W9574V

The dataset's published reuse terms permit NOAA NODD data to be used as
desired. They request attribution, prohibit implying NOAA endorsement or
affiliation, and require modified data not to be presented as unaltered NOAA
data. Terms checked on 5 November 2026:
https://registry.opendata.aws/noaa-nexrad/

This is a modified dataset, not an official NOAA or NWS product. Py-ART was
used to interpolate reflectivity onto a Cartesian grid, crop its extent,
mask missing coverage and levels below radar altitude, and quantize values
to 0.5 dBZ steps. Metadata records the original file, source checksum,
projection, encoding, software versions, and processing details.

Py-ART citation: Helmus and Collis (2016), doi:10.5334/jors.119.
The application code's BSD-3-Clause license is separate from these data terms.

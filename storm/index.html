Developer notes

One archived scan, prepared once as a small 3D image. The browser uses VTK.js volume rendering and an image slice.

The map and volume share a WebGL canvas. The slice has its own small canvas. Drag the map to pan; right-drag to tilt.

Height is exaggerated three times. Missing radar samples are transparent. This is a gridded historical observation.
